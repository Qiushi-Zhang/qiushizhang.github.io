      <h1>Qiushi Zhang</h1>
      <p class="lead">I am a Ph.D. student at the <a href="https://umd.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Maryland, College Park</a>, co-advised by <a href="https://ruoshiliu.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Ruoshi Liu</a> and <a href="https://fang-haoshu.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Hao-Shu Fang</a>.</p>
      <p>I received my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Carnegie Mellon University</a>, where I was advised by <a href="https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/jeffrey-ichnowski/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Jeff Ichnowski</a>, and my B.S. from <a href="https://www.nyu.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">New York University</a>, where I was advised by <a href="https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/ludovic-righetti" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Ludovic Righetti</a>.</p>
      <p>My research lies at the intersection of <strong>robot learning, control, and hardware</strong>. My goal is to enable robots to learn and reason about physical interaction, ultimately achieving human-level dexterity in manipulation and advancing beyond it. I am also dedicated to developing efficient, low-cost robotic hardware platforms that enable scalable data collection and rigorous experimentation in forceful, contact-rich manipulation.</p>
